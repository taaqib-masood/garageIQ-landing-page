    03

    What to check before you call.

    Your Car and Repair

    Describe your car model, the symptom and the work you need before comparing garages.

    Relevant Experience

    Look for reviews about your car brand and confirm the garage handles your model.

    Service Details

    Read accounts of the repair you need, rather than relying only on an overall rating.

    Written Quotes

    Ask what parts, labour and checks a quote includes. A price mentioned in a review is not your quote.

    Compare Like for Like

    Compare the same scope of work and ask each garage about parts, timing and warranty terms.

    Clear Communication

    Confirm you understand the proposed work and how the garage will seek approval for extra repairs.

    Know the limits
    of review evidence.

    A thin review history gives you less context. Consider the number, detail and dates of reviews together, and ask the garage questions that the reviews leave unanswered.

    How this page works

    Last updated: 8 October 2026
    GarageIQ has not launched. This page collects early-access signup details so we can send a launch notification. The sections below explain what happens when you use it.

    What we collect

    When you join the early-access list we store your email address, whether you said you drive a car or own a garage, and — if you chose one — your emirate. If you typed something into the search box before signing up, we store that text too, because knowing what people are looking for before we launch is useful to us. No signup detail beyond the email address is required.

    Anonymous usage

    We record which parts of this page get used — that the signup form was reached, focused, submitted, and whether it succeeded — along with a random per-tab identifier and the domain that referred you. These records contain no name, no email address and no IP address, and cannot be traced back to your signup.

    Where it goes

    Your details are stored in our database, hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia. Your data therefore leaves the UAE. The key this page uses can only add rows — it cannot read them back — so the signup list cannot be retrieved from your browser or by anyone inspecting this page's code.

    What we do not do

    There are no accounts and no payments. We do not sell, rent or share the list. We do not run advertising or advertising trackers. We do not build a profile of you beyond the few fields above.

    Stored on your device

    Your browser keeps your email address after you sign up, so returning here shows an acknowledgement instead of an empty form; the emirate you picked, so you need not pick it twice; and a per-tab identifier used only to join the anonymous usage records above. Clearing your site data removes all three, and the page still works without them.

    Other services this page uses

    The site is hosted by Vercel. The 3D map further down the page loads a graphics library from cdnjs, and only if you scroll far enough to reach it. Both may see your IP address as a normal part of serving a request.

    Removing yourself

    Email privacy@garageiq.ae and we will delete your row. We do not require a reason and there is no form to fill in. You can also simply ignore the launch email when it arrives.

    Accuracy

    This page does not publish verified garage counts, review totals, ratings or price benchmarks. App screens are prelaunch previews and the map is a geographic illustration, not verified coverage. The garage intelligence the product is being built to provide is not available here yet.

    Full terms

    Proper Terms of Use and a Privacy Policy will be published before launch, once the operating company is registered and the wording has been reviewed by a UAE-qualified lawyer. We would rather show you this short, accurate note now than a long document with blanks in it.
