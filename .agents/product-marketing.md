# Product marketing context — verified 5 October 2026

GarageIQ helps UAE drivers understand garage reviews and compare options before
choosing whom to contact. The public site is prelaunch and collects early-access
waitlist interest. Drivers are the primary audience; garage owners are a
secondary waitlist persona. The conversion is a genuinely new driver signup,
not a button click or a duplicate already on the list.

Public canonical: https://www.garageiq.ae/. The page discusses review intelligence,
garage discovery and UAE coverage. Existing numeric coverage/review counts are
page claims awaiting independent verification; do not repeat them in new content
or schema as verified facts. No substantiated customer testimonials, aggregate
ratings, registered legal entity, physical office or confirmed launch date were
provided. Use the existing prelaunch notice and verified contact addresses.

SEO intent: UAE drivers comparing garages, understanding review patterns and
researching garage selection. Informational guides should explain a useful
decision process and link to the driver waitlist without implying services that
are not live. Methodology comes before a Dubai guide and a fair Google Maps
comparison. Avoid mass service-by-city pages or unsupported "best garage" lists.

Measurement requires an authenticated Search Console/Bing source and read-only
aggregate funnel data. These accounts/access have not been verified. Public HTML
and HTTP checks establish crawlability only; they cannot prove impressions,
indexing, search clicks, conversions or AI citations.
